Gym Coach

Add this page to your Home Screen to get the dumbbell icon.

Open Gym Coach

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Add.

From then on, the icon opens Gym Coach directly.
